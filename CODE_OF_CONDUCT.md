# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be specific and kind. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a motive.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's mail, addresses or credentials.
- Accept that a maintainer may close a change that does not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and any
space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through the repository profile or GitHub's
private reporting. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a
warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
